# AMS card

## Description

The AMS Card shows the information about your AMS devices

## Configuration

All configuration should be done via the dashboard card editor. The configuration options available are:

- Card Title
- Custom Sensors
- AMS Selection
- Card Style
- Show/Hide Types

## Vector Card

![Vector ams card](/images/cards/vector-ams-card.png)

- Shows dynamic spool volume remaining if enabled in the slicer, and is a Bambu Filament
  - If there is an MQTT issue where the filament remaining value is not reported correctly, the card will show a full spool
- Active spool can be identified by the highlighted border and animation

## AMS 3H

The H3C's AMS 3H uses the same card. It always uses the vector style, since there is no AMS 3H artwork for the graphic style.

| Drying | Not drying |
|---|---|
| ![AMS 3H in the AMS card while its dry box is drying](/images/readme/ams-card-ams3h-drying.png) | ![AMS 3H in the AMS card with its dry box idle](/images/readme/ams-card-ams3h-idle.png) |

- Its nine spools are laid out like the unit. The back row has two spools beside the **dry box**, which is outlined around its two spools, and the five front spools are below.
- The dry box shows its temperature and humidity above its spools. While it's drying, it also shows the time left and its outline turns orange.
- The AMS 3H has no single humidity or temperature reading, so the info bar shows the other drying zone's humidity and temperature. Tap any reading to open its history.
- The dry box is the drying zone with the fewest trays. The printer doesn't report which tray sits where, so the front row takes the five lowest-numbered of the other trays and the back row the rest.
- The card editor offers a custom remaining sensor for each of the nine trays.

The zone readings come from the AMS 3H's zone sensors (Zone *n* temperature, humidity and remaining drying time). Each one has `zone` (1-based), `zone_id` (the printer's 0-based id) and `slots` (the 1-based tray numbers in the zone) attributes.
